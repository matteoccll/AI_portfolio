About

Hi, I'm Egle.

I grew up in Lithuania, and I've been a massage therapist since 2012 — first qualifying in Swedish massage, then taking the ITEC Level 3 Diploma in Sports Massage at St Mary's University in Twickenham in 2018, and adding advanced training in On-Site Chair Massage at the London School of Massage.

The thing that drew me in was simple: the power of human touch is wildly underestimated. After every massage I'd ever received, I came away calmer, lighter, more able to carry the rest of the week. I wanted to bring that to people who don't usually get to a spa — desk workers running on caffeine, residents in care homes, autistic clients who need a calmer, more predictable environment than a high-street treatment room can offer.

Every session starts the same way: what does this person actually need today? Sometimes it's a deep, focused hour. Sometimes it's ten minutes of hand massage and a cup of tea. Both count.

Common questions

The questions HR teams and care managers ask most.

What space do you actually need on-site?

For corporate chair massage: a quiet-ish corner, a plug socket, and about 2m × 2m. A meeting room is ideal but not essential. For care settings: usually the resident or client's own room, or a familiar sensory space. We bring everything else.

Are you DBS checked and insured?

Yes — Enhanced DBS, full public liability and professional indemnity insurance. Happy to share copies before the first visit; many of our care partners hold them on file.

Can a parent, carer or key worker stay during a session?

Always. For many of our SEN and care home clients that's the whole point — the familiar person stays, we adapt around the routine they already know.

What if a resident or client has a wobbly day?

We slow right down or stop. The session can become a chat, a hand massage, or just sitting in the same room. Wobbly days are written into our approach — they're not a failure of the booking.

What areas of London do you cover?

Greater London as standard. For travel further afield we agree a flat fee with you in writing first. Recurring care partnerships outside Greater London are possible — ask us.

How does invoicing work?

Proper invoices with VAT details, suitable for HR / finance / case-managed budgets. Block bookings can be invoiced upfront or per visit, whichever fits your accounting.

Do you offer pre-visit information for residents or autistic clients?

Yes. Before our first visit you receive a short photo book by email or post: a picture of Egle, of the chair / kit / oils, and a step-by-step of what the first session will look like. Available in Easy Read, large print, or audio on request.
