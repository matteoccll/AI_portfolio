Our story

Every great story starts with a dream.

He mastered it.

And then he opened
Two Little Giraffes.

Niko left Rome with his partner, a suitcase full of dreams and an unshakeable belief that London was where their story was meant to be written.

Like so many who arrive in a new city, they started from scratch. No shortcuts. No safety net. Just two people determined to build something real through hard work and genuine belief.

Niko threw himself into the world of business. He found his feet, learned his craft and quietly built the foundation for something he truly believed in. It was during this time that his passion for authentic Italian gelato took hold. He went to work in a gelato shop. Not as a shortcut. Not as a stopgap. As a student. He showed up every morning and truly learned what it meant to make gelato the Italian way. With patience. With precision. With love for the craft above everything else.

Inside the shop

Step through the door
and you will feel it immediately.

The smell of fresh coffee and handcrafted gelato. Walls alive with the work of local community artists. Artisan goods carefully chosen from independent businesses lining the shelves. And Niko behind the counter, making everything from scratch the way he always has.

This is not just a café. It is a space where people come to slow down, connect, and feel genuinely welcome.

Every month we open our walls to a local artist. We give their work a home and our community something beautiful to discover. Always free. Always open. Always for everyone.

We stock handpicked artisan goods from local independent businesses, because we believe in communities that look after each other.

And at the heart of it all, Niko's gelato. Made fresh every single morning, with real ingredients, less processed sugar than conventional ice cream, and more natural flavour than anything you have tasted before.

Two Little Giraffes is a safe space. A community hub. A little piece of Italy in the heart of Battersea.

Why we built it

A gelato cart shouldn't run on a generator.

Most mobile gelato carts run on a noisy diesel generator, or a freezer that defrosts halfway through the night. The POZZETTI doesn't. Built in Austria around a single lithium battery, it brings the gelateria with it — silent, clean, and good for a full day's service.

  • Ten hours off-grid on one overnight charge — motor, cooling and water all from the same battery.
  • A proper gelateria, on wheels — eight flavours on display, six in reserve, all held at gelato-counter temperature.

Frequently asked

The questions we hear most.

Why do you only have six flavours?
Because everything is made fresh, every morning, from real ingredients. Six is what one family can do properly. More than that, and something gets cut — and we don't cut.
How often do the seasonal flavours change?
Roughly every month, sometimes faster — it depends on what's in season and what we can get from our suppliers. Sign up to the newsletter and you'll know first.
Can I book the gelato bike for my event?
Yes — visit the gelato bike page for details and to send us an enquiry. We're usually booked 2–6 weeks ahead.
Do you offer dairy-free options?
Almost always. At least two of our six flavours are dairy-free at any given moment — usually a fruit sorbetto and an oat-milk gelato.
Are you open in winter?
Yes — gelato is for every season. Winter flavours lean towards chocolate, hazelnut, chestnut and ricotta-based gelatos. Summer is when fruits and sorbetti take over.
I'm an artist — how do I exhibit?
Have a look at our art page for the programme. We're usually booked three months out. Submissions are open all year.
Do you do catering / wholesale?
For events, our gelato bike is the answer — see the bike page. We don't currently do wholesale or supply to other businesses; the shop is small and we want to keep it that way.
